Questions

Common questions.

Why is our office Wi-Fi slow in JLT?

Towers have dozens of networks on the same channels. A Wi-Fi Checkup finds the interference and dead zones, and the fix is often better channels, the right access point positions or a cable, not a new internet package.

Can you get a building work permit?

Yes. Many JLT towers ask for a work permit for cabling or installation. We prepare what the building needs and work in the hours they allow.

Do you support free-zone companies?

Yes. We look after small companies of any licence type, with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace email, laptops, backup and security.

How fast can you come?

Usually within a day for support jobs. Installations are booked after the free site visit.
