What we do

A barrier is only as good as its installation and how it decides to open.

Barrier supply and installation

Remote control

Number-plate recognition

Questions

Common questions.

Remote, card, UHF tag or number plate: which is best?

Remotes suit villas and small car parks. Cards are cheap per user. UHF tags let residents drive in without stopping. Number-plate recognition needs nothing in the car and keeps a record of every entry.

Do barriers need maintenance?

Yes. We recommend a check every few months: arm balance, motor, loop tuning and safety sensors. It prevents most breakdowns.

What stops the arm from hitting a car?

Loop detectors in the road and photocell sensors keep the arm open while a car is under it.

Can you repair a barrier installed by someone else?

Yes. We service and repair most barrier brands, including arms, motors, controllers and loops.
