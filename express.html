Questions

Good to know

How long does a repair take?

Software jobs like a Windows reinstall or virus clean-up are usually done the same day. Screens, batteries and other parts depend on the part: most are done in 1 to 3 days. Your tracking page always shows the expected date.

Do I pay if I decide not to repair?

The check on a visit is free. If we picked it up and you decide not to go ahead, you only pay the pickup and check fee. We tell you this before we collect.

Will I lose my files?

Not if we can help it. Tell us if your files matter and we back them up before any software work. If the drive is failing, we tell you first and quote data recovery separately.

Which devices do you repair?

Windows laptops and desktops, MacBooks and iMacs, iPhone and Android phones, and tablets. For business laptops we can also set up email, Office, printers and backup.

How do I follow my repair?

After booking you get a code like QT-7K4M-2PXA. Open the tracking page, type the code and you'll see each step: received, checking, price approval, repairing and ready.

Need it today?

Message us now. If it's a quick fix, we can often do it on the same day at your place.
