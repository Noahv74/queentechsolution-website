How we work it out

Simple rules, real prices.

Cameras

Areas outside use bullet cameras built for sun and dust. Areas inside use small dome cameras. Colour-at-night cameras are used outside when you tick that option.

Recorder

We pick the smallest recorder with enough channels: 4, 8 or 16, and move up to the 16-channel model when you need room for a second disk. These recorders power the cameras through the network cable (PoE), so no extra power points are needed at each camera.

Storage

About 2.2 Mbps for a 2MP camera and 3.9 Mbps for 4MP on H.265, recording 24/7. Motion-only recording is counted at about 40% of that. We add 10% for the recorder's file system. Busy scenes use more.

Prices

Equipment prices are the lowest we found at UAE shops on –. Installation is our own price list, including cabling, mounting, testing and phone app setup. The final price is confirmed after a free site visit.
