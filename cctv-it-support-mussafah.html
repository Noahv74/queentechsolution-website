Questions

Common questions.

Do you really come to Mussafah from Dubai?

Yes. We plan Abu Dhabi work on fixed days, and remote help works any day. Message us and we'll give you the next visit date.

Can attendance reports go to our payroll?

Yes. Face terminals export daily in and out times to Excel, ready for payroll.

Our yard is big. How many cameras do we need?

It depends on the yard and gates. Try the camera planner for an estimate, then we confirm it at the free site visit.

Can the barrier open for our company vehicles only?

Yes, with windshield tags or a number-plate camera. Visitors can use a button at the guard room.
