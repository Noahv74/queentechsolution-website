Every visit

What we check

A full checklist on every visit. You get a short report on WhatsApp after each one, with photos of anything that needs attention.

Cameras

  • Clean the lens and housing
  • Check focus, angle and night vision
  • Check for water, dust or spider webs
  • Re-aim any camera that moved

Recorder (NVR / DVR)

  • Hard-disk health and free space
  • Confirm how many days are recorded
  • Correct date and time on footage
  • Firmware update when it's safe

Cabling and power

  • Connectors, joints and cable runs
  • PoE switch and power supplies
  • UPS battery test if you have one
  • Fix loose or damaged cables found

Remote viewing

  • App working on your phones
  • Add or remove users
  • Change weak or default passwords
  • Test alerts and playback

Questions

Good to know

Do you maintain systems you didn't install?

Yes. On the first visit we check the whole system and tell you if anything needs fixing before the plan starts. You need the recorder's admin password, or we can help you reset it.

What if a camera breaks between visits?

Message us. Care Plan customers get priority visits. If a part needs replacing, we send you the price first and you get 10% off.

I have more cameras than the plan covers.

Every plan can take extra cameras for a small fee per camera per year. For large buildings and compounds we make a custom plan.

Can I pay monthly?

Plans are yearly. For businesses we can split the payment into quarters.
