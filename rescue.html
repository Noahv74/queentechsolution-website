    Good habits

    Fewer errors, every day.

    Drivers from the maker

    Get graphics drivers only from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel. Driver "updater" apps often install the wrong one, or adware.

    No random DLL files

    When a game says a DLL file is missing, install the Microsoft package it belongs to. DLL download sites are a common way to get malware.

    Keep it cool

    Dust and old thermal paste make a PC throttle and crash. A clean and fresh paste every year or two keeps FPS steady.

    One overlay at a time

    Discord, Steam, Xbox Game Bar and GPU overlays all hook into the game. Turning off the ones you don't use stops many crashes.
