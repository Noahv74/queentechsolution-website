Quick checks before you message us

These fix a lot of common problems in a few minutes. If they don't, pick your problem above.

Game crashes or won't open

  1. Restart the PC, then open the game again.
  2. Steam: right-click the game › Properties › Installed Files › Verify integrity of game files.
  3. Epic Games: the game's menu (…) › Manage › Verify.
  4. Install the latest graphics driver from NVIDIA or AMD.

Low FPS or stutter

  1. Laptop: plug in the charger. Many laptops slow down on battery.
  2. Windows: Settings › Gaming › turn Game Mode on.
  3. Close browsers and apps running in the background.
  4. Lower the resolution or graphics preset in the game.

High ping or lag online

  1. Use a LAN cable instead of WiFi if you can.
  2. Restart your router.
  3. Stop downloads and updates on other devices while you play.
  4. Choose the game server closest to the UAE.

PC very hot or loud

  1. Make sure the vents are not blocked.
  2. Laptops: use them on a desk, not on a bed or sofa.
  3. If the PC shuts down while gaming, it is often heat. Stop using it hard and book a cleaning.
