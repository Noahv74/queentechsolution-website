Questions

Common questions.

Do you charge extra to come to Abu Dhabi?

The site visit is free. Tell us your area when you message us and we confirm the visit time and the full price in writing before any work.

Which cameras do you install?

Mostly Hikvision and Dahua IP cameras, 2MP or 4MP, with colour-at-night models for outdoor areas. We can quote other brands on request.

Can you cover a large yard or warehouse?

Yes. We plan longer cable runs, extra recorders and larger disks for big sites, and use colour-at-night cameras where lighting is poor.

Can you service an existing system?

Yes. We repair cameras, replace failed disks, update recorders and add cameras to systems installed by others.
