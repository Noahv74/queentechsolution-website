Questions

Good to know

Card, fingerprint or face?

Cards are the cheapest but can be shared or lost. Fingerprint stops sharing and gives you an attendance report. Face is touch-free and fastest at busy doors, and works well for offices and clinics.

What happens in a power cut or a fire?

Each door has a power supply with a backup battery. Magnetic locks open when power is cut, and the green break glass opens the door at once in an emergency, as fire safety requires.

Tags or number-plate camera for the car park?

Windshield tags are cheap per car and very reliable. A number-plate camera needs no tags, so it suits visitors and changing tenants, but the camera costs more.

Can I see who came in from my phone?

Yes. Most systems we install have an app or software that shows entries and attendance, and you can add or remove people any time.

Want the exact price?

We visit, check the doors and the road, and give you a fixed quote. The visit is free.
