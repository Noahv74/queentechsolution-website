Questions

Common questions.

How do I see faces and plates at night in the yard?

We use colour-at-night or strong infrared cameras placed at the right height and angle, and a separate camera for plates at the gate.

Can workers cheat the attendance system?

Not with face recognition. Cards can be shared, fingerprints wear off with heavy work, so for workshops we usually recommend face terminals.

Can a barrier open for our trucks only?

Yes. Windshield tags or a number-plate camera open it for listed vehicles, and a remote or button for visitors.

How many days of recording do warehouses keep?

Most keep 30 days. Insurance or clients sometimes ask for more. We size the disks to match.
