Questions

Common questions.

My bedroom has no Wi-Fi. Do I need a new internet package?

Usually not. The router is in one corner and walls block the signal. Two or three mesh units, or one cable to a second access point, fix it on the same line.

Can you install cameras without drilling?

Yes. Many indoor cameras stand on a shelf and use Wi-Fi. For video doorbells we check what the building allows first.

Can you collect my laptop from my building?

Yes. Book on the ExpressFix page and choose pickup. You can track the repair online.

Do you do holiday-home setups?

Yes. Smart locks or keypad access for check-in, Wi-Fi for guests, and an entrance camera that respects guests' privacy inside.
