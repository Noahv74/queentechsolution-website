On the visit

What we do

  1. Walk-through and signal mapWe measure the Wi-Fi signal in every room, corner and floor and mark the dead zones on your floor plan.
  2. Speed testsWe test the internet speed at the router and in the rooms you use most, so you can see where the speed drops.
  3. Interference scanWe check which channels your neighbours use and what blocks the signal: thick walls, mirrors, metal, other networks.
  4. Router and line checkWe check your router, the du or e& line, cables and any extenders already in place.
  5. Your recommended setupYou get a short report: where to put access points or mesh units, which cables are needed, and a fixed price to do it.

Quick fixes to try first

  • Restart the router and any extenders.
  • Move the router out of cupboards and away from the TV.
  • Use 5 GHz near the router and 2.4 GHz far away.
  • Test with a cable. If it's slow on a cable too, the problem is the line, not the Wi-Fi.

Questions

Good to know

How long is the visit?

About 1 hour for an apartment and 2 to 3 hours for a large villa or office floor. You get the report the same day.

Do I have to use you for the installation?

No. The report is yours to keep. If you do use us, the checkup fee comes off the installation price.

Will you just tell me to buy an expensive system?

No. Often moving the router, changing a channel or adding one cable fixes it. We recommend the simplest setup that covers your space.

Can you fix it on the same visit?

Small fixes like router settings and channels, yes. If you need new access points or cabling, we quote first and come back with the equipment.
