Questions

Common questions.

How many cameras does my villa or shop need?

Most villas need 6 to 10 cameras and most shops 3 to 5. It depends on entrances, outside walls and parking. Try our free camera planner for a quick estimate, then we confirm it at the free site visit.

How long are recordings kept?

It depends on the number of cameras, their resolution and the disk size. We usually plan for 30 days, and can go to 60 or 90 days with larger disks.

Can I watch the cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the Hikvision or Dahua app on your phone, and on other phones you choose, for live view and playback from anywhere.

Does my business need SIRA-approved CCTV?

Some businesses in Dubai must follow SIRA rules for their security systems. Tell us your business type and we'll plan with that in mind and tell you honestly if you need a SIRA-approved contractor for your activity.

Can you fix a system someone else installed?

Yes. We repair, extend and upgrade existing Hikvision, Dahua and other systems, including old analogue cameras.
