Questions

Common questions.

Can you keep my old cameras?

Often yes. Analogue cameras can work with a new hybrid recorder, so you change only what's needed. We tell you honestly at the site visit.

Can I watch all my shops on one phone?

Yes. We add every shop's recorder to one app, so you can switch between branches.

My recorder stopped recording. What happened?

Usually the hard disk is full or failing, or the date was reset after a power cut. We check it and replace the disk if needed.

Do you work late, after the shop closes?

Yes. We're open every day until 10 pm and plan installation around your opening hours.
