Questions

Common questions.

Do you repair both iPhone and Android?

Yes. We repair iPhone, Samsung and most Android phones. Tell us your model so we can confirm the part is available.

Will my data be safe?

Screen and battery repairs don't touch your data. For software repairs we'll explain first whether a reset is needed, and back up when possible.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the part and model. We tell you the expected time together with the price.

Can you move WhatsApp to a new phone?

Yes. We help move WhatsApp chats, photos and contacts to your new phone, including from Android to iPhone where the apps support it.
