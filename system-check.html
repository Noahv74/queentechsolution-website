Your privacy

Safe to run

  • Passwords are used only during the check. They are never saved in the report or sent anywhere.
  • It only looks at the local network this PC is on, and any IP range you add.

Questions

Good to know

Why does Windows warn me?

The tool is a script, not a signed app, so Windows SmartScreen asks before running it. Click More info → Run anyway. You can open it in Notepad first to check it.

Which brands can it read?

Hikvision and Dahua recorders and cameras in full, most other ONVIF devices (model, serial, firmware), and managed switches that have SNMP turned on. Everything else still appears in the device list.

Some cameras are missing from the list.

Cameras plugged into the recorder's own PoE ports use a private network inside the NVR and do not show on your LAN. Log in to the recorder and they appear under its channels.

Do I need the passwords?

No. Without them you still get every device and IP. With them the report shows channels, disks, firmware and offline cameras.

The check is free. We use the report to prepare your BOQ and quote, with no obligation.
