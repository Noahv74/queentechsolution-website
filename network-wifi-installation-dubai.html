Questions

Common questions.

Why is my WiFi slow in some rooms?

Walls, distance and too many devices on one router are the usual causes. Adding the right number of access points or a mesh system, placed properly, fixes most cases.

Do I need cables if I have WiFi?

For desks, TVs, consoles and cameras, a cable is faster and more reliable. We usually combine network points for fixed devices with good WiFi for everything else.

Can guests use WiFi without accessing our files?

Yes. We set up a separate guest network that only gives internet access.

Can you work after office hours?

Yes. We can plan cabling and setup in the evening or at the weekend so your work isn't interrupted.
