How we calculate

Estimates, not guesses.

CCTV

Typical bitrates

We start from common recorder defaults at 25 fps on H.264 (about 4 Mbps for 2MP, 6 for 4MP, 7 for 5MP, 10 for 8MP), then adjust for compression, frame rate and movement. Storage adds about 10% for the recorder's file system.

Office

Per-activity usage

Each activity uses a typical amount of speed, for example around 4 Mbps down and 3 Mbps up for an HD video call. We add them up for the busiest hour and add spare capacity so things don't slow down at peak time.

Transfers

Bits, not bytes

Internet speed is sold in megabits; files are measured in megabytes. One byte is 8 bits, so a 100 Mbps line moves about 12 MB a second at best, and a little less in practice.

Real numbers depend on the exact cameras, lighting, night-time IR noise and your provider. For a figure you can rely on, we check your site and equipment for free.
